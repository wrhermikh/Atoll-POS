Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Atoll POS ("we", "us") provides a point-of-sale and shop management service for shops in the Maldives, available at atollpos.vercel.app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Shop details: shop name, address, phone, GST TIN and the settings you choose.
  • Staff details: the names, roles and permissions of the people you add, and the automatic sign-in IDs we create for them.
  • Business records you enter: products, prices, stock, suppliers, customers (name, phone, balances), invoices, quotations, payments, expenses and cash book entries.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information and activity logs needed to keep the service secure and working. A copy of your shop data is also kept on your device so the app can work offline.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and run the service, including sign-in, saving your records and syncing between your devices.
  • To manage your free trial, plan and payments, and to provide support.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Your customers' information

The customer and sales records you enter belong to your business. We only process them on your behalf to provide the service. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to record your customers' details and for using them properly.

4. Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we use Google only to confirm who you are (name and email). Our use of information received from Google is limited to providing this sign-in.

5. Where your data is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting). They process data only to provide those services to us. Within your shop, access is limited by the roles and permissions you give your staff. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and per-shop separation of data. No online service is completely risk-free, so please use strong passwords and keep staff access limited to what each person needs.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your shop and its data at any time. We will do so within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days, except for records we are required by law to keep. You can also export your invoices and reports from the app before you leave.

8. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. Shop owners can edit or remove staff and customer records in the app.

9. Children

The service is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make important changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
